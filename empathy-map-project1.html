The Mindset Shift — From Designer → Researcher
🔬 Investigate: Micro-Workouts

5-minute activity windows scattered through the day. A 5-min gap between meetings = a micro-workout opportunity. No gym. No gear. No excuses.

5 min→ Activity→ 5 min
🪑 Research: Desk Exercises

Seated stretches, posture corrections, desk-bound movement. Solve for the user who cannot leave their desk — not the user who lives at the gym.

Synthesis · Key Insights & Design Direction

The Problem Statement
& How Might We.

Translating the empathy map into actionable design questions.

Core Problem Statement
Arjun, a busy 29-year-old professional, needs a way to integrate micro-moments of movement into his workday because he feels guilty, stiff, and burnout — yet every existing fitness app demands time, equipment, and effort he simply does not have.
How Might We Questions — Design Prompts
01
Reduce frictionHow might we make starting a workout require zero decision-making — launchable in under 3 seconds?
02
Fit the scheduleHow might we detect or suggest activity during natural 5-min gaps in a meeting-heavy calendar?
03
Eliminate guiltHow might we reframe "I skipped today" into a positive nudge rather than a broken streak?
04
Go gym-freeHow might we design every workout to be completable from a desk chair, in office clothes?
05
Calm the UIHow might we design an interface so quiet and focused that it feels like a relief, not another app?
06
Prove value fastHow might we let users feel a tangible benefit (less neck pain, clearer head) within their very first session?
